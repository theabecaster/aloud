# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mac users who write throughout the day and want to speak naturally instead of typing. They work across chat, email, notes, browsers, terminals, and code editors, and need dictation to feel immediate, private, dependable, and out of the way.

## Product Purpose

Aloud is a menu bar dictation utility: hold a key, speak, release, and the words appear at the current cursor. It succeeds when dictation is faster than typing, easy to recover or reuse, fully understandable at a glance, and trusted because speech, transcripts, settings, and history remain on the Mac.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, capable, trustworthy. The product should feel like a thoughtful part of macOS: clear enough for a first-time user, efficient enough to disappear into an expert workflow, and warm without becoming decorative.

## Anti-references

Avoid cloud-service dashboards, promotional upgrade surfaces, ornamental glass effects, dense power-user chrome, playful controls that obscure standard macOS behavior, and interfaces that expose transcription-engine implementation details. Craft is a reference for hierarchy, spatial calm, and sidebar polish only. Monologue is a reference for glanceable recent dictations only.

## Design Principles

- Keep the words one gesture away.
- Make local privacy visible through plain, useful cues.
- Use native behavior so the interface feels learned before it is used.
- Reveal secondary actions at the moment they become relevant.
- Prefer calm hierarchy and useful density over decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow current Apple platform conventions for keyboard navigation, VoiceOver labels, full-size hit targets, system contrast, Dynamic Type-equivalent macOS text styles, localization, reduced motion, and non-hover alternatives for every hover-revealed action.
